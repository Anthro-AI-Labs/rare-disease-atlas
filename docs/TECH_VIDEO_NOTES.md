# Tech video notes (5 bullets, numbers from `graph.json` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`)

1. **Cache-first, reproducible pipeline.** One `make graph` rebuilds everything: 12 diseases and 252 HPO edges become a 748-node, 1,390-edge graph (550 curated, 91 manual, 35 computed, 714 LLM-extracted). Every PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov and OpenAI call is cached by key, so a rerun takes about a second and never spends tokens. Edges carry source, references, confidence and status, validated by a pydantic schema on every export.
2. **Verbatim is not the same as supported (our biggest catch).** Exact-quote checking dropped 45 of 759 extracted claims (5.9%). But a lead review showed a quote can be real and still not say the claim, so we added a second, span-only entailment call: of 506 directional claims, 362 were "yes", 32 "partial", 112 "no" (22% turned into context only). The check also changed the population for 277 claims, and the top confidence tier shrank from 123 to 35 claims. The STXBP1 explanation now cites only claims whose quote states the effect.
3. **What didn't work, and what we did instead.** A joint effect×function overlap gave scores below 0.15 everywhere, so we switched to marginals and wrote that down. Review papers made "shared authors" look real (120 flagged, 81 after requiring two different papers for two different genes). Our validator once rejected "rather than a loss of function" as an assertion. The benign SCN2A and SCN8A forms have about one directional claim each; we report that as a finding and refused to tune weights to make clusters pass.
4. **Honesty as a feature.** Computed links are always "hypothesis"; a route is "supported" only when a verified shared study and verified patient groups back it: 4 of 12 diseases qualify today (STXBP1, SYNGAP1, SCN2A DEE11, SCN8A DEE13) and the other 8 say "Route incomplete" and name the missing part. Explanations are AI-written but gated: edge ids must come from the route, no invented identifiers, loss/gain-of-function statements need an entailed claim (12 of 12 passed first try). Conflicting evidence is shown with both sides and never resolved by us.
5. **Tools.** Built end to end with Claude Code (plan mode, a test-first loop, 61 pytest tests, browser-pane visual QA) and OpenAI structured outputs (gpt-5.4-mini for extraction, entailment and explanations, always via `OPENAI_MODEL`, server-side only). Data from HPO, MONDO, PubMed E-utilities and ClinicalTrials.gov; Next.js 16 (App Router, Tailwind) on Vercel.
